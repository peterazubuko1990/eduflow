About Human Resource

The main function of the human resource person is to make necessary staff arrangements, maintain staff attendance for the school.Human Resource module workflow: First we will add Department, Designation, Leave Type then we will add staff member from Staff Directory, day by day Staff Attendance, and Payroll, Apply Leave, Approve Leave Request, Teacher Rating, Disabled Staff.
